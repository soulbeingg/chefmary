The Culinary Journey

Chef Mary: Excellence In Every Detail.

Currently serving as the Head Chef at Radisson Blu, Mary's culinary path began with a deep-rooted passion for the vibrant flavors of Kenya. Her career has taken her through some of the most prestigious kitchens in Nairobi, refining a style that balances technical French precision with the soul of Pan-African cuisine.

She believes that the best dishes are those that tell a story—of the earth, the season, and the hands that harvested the ingredients.
